A · Natural math worksheets

Write it once. Read it like a hand calculation.

Definitions use :=, results use =. Fractions, roots, subscripts, Greek letters and matrices look the way they do on paper - and the units come along.

Units in every line

  • SI, US and CGS units with prefixes from pico to tera
  • Show any result in the unit you want: N, kN or lbf
  • Mixed units add up correctly: 2 m + 30 cm = 2.3 m
  • °C, °F and decibels (dB, dBm, dBV)
  • Your own units and your own functions
  • A unit mistake turns the line red and says why

Better than a calculator or spreadsheet because…

the equation, its inputs and its answer are all visible, in order, with units. Anyone can check the sheet - including you, six months later.

B · Engineering graphs

Plots that live next to the math.

Every graph is drawn from the equations above it and redraws when they change. Zoom, trace with a cursor, and add markers.

Bode plots with crossover and phase margin

Type the transfer function in s. MathSheet draws gain and phase against a log frequency axis, finds where the gain crosses 0 dB, and labels the crossover frequency and phase margin.

Example: buck converter, voltage mode, Type III compensator - crossover 33.65 kHz, phase margin 55.9°.

Also: polar plots, parametric curves, 3D bars, points and curves in space, animation saved as a video.

C · Solving and analysis

Worked examples, solved on the page.

Roots, systems of equations, best designs, matrix unknowns and complex numbers - each with units where they belong.

Solve block

Where does a diode settle from 5 V?

The problem
A diode (Is = 1 pA, VT = 25 mV) and a 1 kΩ resistor across 5 V. The diode equation is exponential, so there is no simple formula.
In MathSheet
Give a guess, write both equations under Given with a bold equals, then Find(VD, ID).
The result
VD = 0.555 V, ID = 4.445 mA - units included.

Optimization

The 1-liter can that uses the least metal

The problem
Find the radius and height of a closed can holding 1 liter with the smallest surface area.
In MathSheet
Write the area A(r, h), put the volume condition under Given, then Minimize(A, r, h).
The result
r = 5.419 cm, h = 10.839 cm, A = 553.581 cm² - the best can is as tall as it is wide.

Matrix unknowns

The Riccati equation for state feedback

The problem
For x′ = A·x + B·u, find the matrix P that solves ATP + PA − PBBTP + Q = 0, and the feedback gain K.
In MathSheet
A whole matrix can be the unknown: guess P as an identity matrix, write the equation under Given, Find(P).
The result
P = [1.732 1; 1 0.732] (that is √3 and √3 − 1), K = (1, 0.732).

Root with units

When does the capacitor reach 5 V?

The problem
A 1 kΩ / 1 µF circuit charges toward 10 V. When does it reach 5 V?
In MathSheet
root(VC(t) − 5 V, t, 0 s, 5 ms) searches between 0 and 5 ms.
The result
t1 = 0.693 ms, matching the hand formula R·C·ln 2.
More solving tools
  • polyroots: every root of a polynomial
  • Minerr: the closest fit when there is no exact answer (least squares)
  • Conditions such as x > 0 or 1 ≤ x ≤ 3 inside a solve block
  • A solve block used as a function, for example over a range of inputs
  • Complex answers from a complex guess

E · Import

Bring your .xmcd worksheets with you.

File › Import worksheet (.xmcd) opens worksheets saved in the .xmcd format and rebuilds them as live MathSheet worksheets. A report at the top lists what came in and compares the answers saved in the file with MathSheet's own.

What comes across

  • Equations, definitions and units
  • Text, programs, matrices, ranges, sums
  • X-Y plots rebuilt live (the original picture is kept beside each for comparison)
  • Anything it cannot rebuild stays as a picture with a note

Good to know

Only the .xmcd format is supported - not .xmcdz, .mcdx or the older binary .mcd. The original file is never changed. Check the report on every imported sheet: some programs evaluate a few things differently, and the report points to each line that needs a look.
