What happens after the 14-day trial?

MathSheet keeps working as a free viewer: you can still open and print your worksheets. Enter a licence key to edit and save again - your work is never lost.

How do I get my licence key?

Right after you pay, the key is shown on screen and sent to you by e-mail. In MathSheet, choose Help › Licence › Enter licence key and paste it in.
