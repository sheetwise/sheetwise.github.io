Questions about buying

What happens after I pay?

Lemon Squeezy shows your license key on screen and e-mails it to you with your receipt. In MathSheet choose Help › Licence › Enter licence key and paste it in. Your trial copy becomes the full version - nothing to reinstall. Step-by-step: activating a license.

Does MathSheet need the internet?

Only to activate your key the first time. After that MathSheet works offline. About once a month it quietly checks the key when the computer is online; if you are offline, or the license service cannot be reached, MathSheet keeps working.

Can I use it on two computers?

One license is for one computer. If you replace your computer, e-mail us and we will move your license to the new one. A license bought by a company may be used by one person at a time on one computer.

What happens to my worksheets if I do not buy?

They are yours. After the trial MathSheet becomes a viewer: you can still open and print every worksheet.
