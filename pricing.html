Questions about buying

What happens after I sign up?

Lemon Squeezy shows your license key on screen and e-mails it to you with your receipt. In MathSheet choose Help › Licence › Enter licence key and paste it in. MathSheet becomes the full version - nothing to reinstall. Step-by-step: activating a license.

Does MathSheet need the internet?

Only to activate your key the first time. After that MathSheet works offline. About once a month it quietly checks the key when the computer is online; if you are offline, or the license service cannot be reached, MathSheet keeps working.

What happens after the first year?

Renewal may be free or paid - we reserve the right to charge for MathSheet after the free year. Nothing is charged automatically; we do not even have a card. Before your year ends we e-mail you whether renewal is free or its price. If you do not continue, MathSheet becomes a viewer: your worksheets still open and print.

Can I use it on two computers?

One license is for one computer. If you replace your computer, e-mail us and we will move your license to the new one. A license bought by a company may be used by one person at a time on one computer.

What happens to my worksheets if I stop?

They are yours. Without a current license MathSheet becomes a viewer: you can still open and print every worksheet.

Who handles the payment?

Lemon Squeezy, our reseller and merchant of record. They take the payment, handle sales tax and send the receipt. We never see your card number.

Can I get a refund?

Your first year is free, so there is nothing to refund. For anything you pay later: yes, within 30 days. See the refund policy.

Are updates included?

Any update we offer will say whether it is free for existing license holders. We do not promise future features or versions.
