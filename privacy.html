Privacy policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This policy explains what information Sheetwise Engineering LLC ("we") collects when you use MathSheet or this website. In short: very little.

Your worksheets

MathSheet works offline. Your worksheets, calculations and files stay on your computer. We never receive them.

License activation

MathSheet goes online only to activate a license key and, about once a month, to check quietly that the key is still valid. Each time it sends only:

  • your license key, and
  • a computer code - a short code made from your computer's hardware, so a key works on one computer. It does not contain your name, your files or other personal information.

These checks go to the license service of Lemon Squeezy, our reseller. If you are offline or the service cannot be reached, MathSheet keeps working.

Buying MathSheet

Purchases are handled by Lemon Squeezy as merchant of record. They collect your name, e-mail address, billing details and payment information to process the order, under their own privacy policy (lemonsqueezy.com/privacy). We receive your name, e-mail address, country and order details so we can support you. We never see your full card number.

E-mails you send us

If you e-mail us, we keep your message and address to answer you. We do not sell or share it.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Its one script only switches the Buy and Download buttons on and shows pictures full size. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security. The Buy button takes you to the Lemon Squeezy checkout, which has its own policy.

Your choices

You can ask what information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by e-mailing sheetwiseengllc@gmail.com. We keep order records as long as tax law requires.

Children

MathSheet is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
