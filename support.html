Support and contact

Help inside MathSheet

Press F1 in MathSheet for Help. It has a topic and a lesson for every feature, a search box, and an "Explain this error" button on any red line. Help > User manual opens the full manual as a PDF, and Help > Example sheets has worked examples you can open and change.

Common questions

I have a new computer

On the old computer choose Help › Licence › Move licence, then enter the same key on the new one. If the old computer is broken, e-mail us with your order number.

I lost my licence key

Look for the receipt e-mail from Lemon Squeezy, or e-mail us with the address you used to buy.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC"

Click More info, check the publisher is Sheetwise Engineering LLC, then click Run anyway.
